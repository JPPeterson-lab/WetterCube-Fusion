## v0.9.9-beta9 (2026-10-23)

### New
- **7-day forecast:** A new page between the main screen and the temperature history shows the next seven days side by side: weekday and date, weather icon, high and low (coloured by temperature), rain amount with probability and the strongest wind of the day. The data comes from Open-Meteo and is refreshed hourly. You reach the page with the arrow on the right of the main screen.
