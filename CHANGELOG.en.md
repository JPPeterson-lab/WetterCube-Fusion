## v1.0.0-rc13 (2026-11-05)

### Fixed
- **Update window not visible:** When an update was started from the web interface or WetterCube Control, the display could stay dimmed or be covered by the picture frame, the night clock or a warning screen, so the running update could not be seen. The screen now stays bright during the update and nothing switches away from the update window.
